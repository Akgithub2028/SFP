\paragraph{SmolLM2-135M-Instruct.}
135M parameters, $L = 30$ layers, probe layers at 7/15/22, activation dim $d = 576$.
Baseline: 0\% math accuracy, 1.177 math perplexity.

\paragraph{Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct.}
1.5B parameters, $L = 28$ layers, probe layers at 7/14/21, activation dim $d = 1536$.
Loads in bfloat16; all hooks preserve original dtype.
Baseline: 44.44\% math accuracy, 0.505 math perplexity.

\paragraph{Causal intervention details.}
Noise $\epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2 I_r)$ injected at each probe layer.
$\sigma = s \cdot \bar{\sigma}_{\text{dim}}$ where $s$ is the noise scale multiplier (default 1.0) and $\bar{\sigma}_{\text{dim}}$ is the mean eigenvalue scale ($\sqrt{\text{variance}}$) of the selected PCA dimensions.
Noise generated on CPU, cast to model dtype before injection.

\paragraph{Ablation importance cost.}
$k$ forward passes per layer $\times$ 3 layers $\times$ 20 eval examples = $\sim$7{,}680 forward passes for the 1.5B model ($\sim$10 min on A10G).

\paragraph{Computational cost.}
Full H1 validation (1.5B, 500 steps, 20 checkpoints): $\sim$90 minutes on a single A10G GPU.
Causal intervention test: $\sim$15 minutes additional.
Total cost per experiment: approximately \$2--3 on Modal.
