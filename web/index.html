How it works

1

Add your method

Write a loss function in methods.py. See existing methods for the pattern.

2

Open a PR

Push to your fork and open a pull request.

3

We run the benchmark

A maintainer labels your PR. Modal spins up a GPU and runs 3 seeds automatically.

4

Scores posted

Results appear as a comment on your PR. If you beat the leaderboard, we merge.

Metrics

Score = 0.6 × Retention + 0.4 × Plasticity
Retention = mean accuracy on old tasks after all training (higher = less forgetting)
Plasticity = accuracy on the most recently trained task (higher = better learning)
